## 2026-11-12 — Show a ticket held up by a failing lane as held up, not as Dane's move (#200)

On 11/11 the Mini lost its internet connection for 12.5 hours. The relay
failed every pass, and SP-207 sat on the page looking like Dane's move,
even though he had already said "merge". Nothing on the page explained why
nothing was happening.

Now, when a lane keeps failing, any ticket that lane owes the next move
says so in one amber line at the top of its page, for example "Held up:
Relay keeps failing since 9:00 pm". A ticket Dane has already answered waits
on the relay. A Queued or Rework ticket waits on Build, one In review waits
on Review, and a Requested one waits on Intake. Held-up tickets stay off his
Waiting on You list and the bell. A ticket where a loop has asked him
something is never marked held up, so a real question can't be hidden. As
soon as the lane passes again, the line goes away.
