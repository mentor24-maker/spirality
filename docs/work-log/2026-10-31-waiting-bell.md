## 2026-10-31 — Replace the Waiting on You link with bell icon (#48)

The "Waiting on you" words at the top left of the tracker page are now a bell
icon at the top right, next to "Signed in as Dane". The bell's colour tells
Dane how much is waiting for him without opening anything: grey when there is
nothing, yellow for one to three tickets, red for four or more. It uses the
same count as the number in the browser tab, and it updates whenever he
changes page and once a minute while the tab is left open. Clicking the bell
still opens the Waiting on you list.
