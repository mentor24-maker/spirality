## 2026-11-27 — Tweaks to Task Details page (#353)

When a ticket waits on Dane, the left box of its page used to list everything first (his request, the loop's summary, the whole ask in bold red) and only then, at the bottom, the button he presses. Now the button comes first: Merge, Go, the answer dropdown or the reply box, each with its one-line hint, sit right under the Status line.

The ask below it is easier to read too. Only its headline (the words after "NEEDED FROM DANE:") is still bold red. The pull request's `PR:` line is bold black on a line of its own. On a review card, the steps for checking the work sit under a "Test Instructions" heading in plain black, and the "To merge:" paragraph is plain as well. Cards that have no such steps, like the intake loop's "go" question, just lose the red on everything but their headline. No words were changed, and the phone page and the red box in the comments look the same as before.
