## 2026-11-09 — An alarm on StarCaster's bus reaches nobody until Dane opens the page: decide how loud a bus post should be (#134)

StarCaster's machines post their alarms (a job stopped, the queue stalled) to
"the bus", a Team chat ticket in the tracker. Until now nothing told Dane a
post had arrived; he had to go and look. On 2026-09-16 an alarm sat unread
for ninety hours that way.

Dane chose option D: a text for alarms, and a count on the page for
everything else. Now a post that starts with `ALARM:` is sent to the bus
project's phone number as an iMessage from the Mini, through the same
channel as the hand-over texts. Every post, alarm or not, also shows up as an
orange "New on the Bus" count at the top of every page. Clicking it opens the
Team chat posts, and the count clears for him alone. It is the same on his
phone and his desk, because the tracker remembers what he has seen.

To get texts, the bus project needs his phone number on its Edit project
page, once StarCaster's bus project exists.

Review sent it back once: the real alarm texts ended with the line
"[machine] posted by a loop under Dane's token — not his word", which every
loop comment carries so the loops can tell their words from his. The text now
leaves that line off. And a text that fails to send (Messages not signed in,
say) is now tried again on its own timer, a minute later and then longer, instead
of waiting for the next thing to happen on the tracker, which at night can be
hours away.
