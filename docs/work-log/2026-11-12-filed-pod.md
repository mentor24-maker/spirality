## 2026-11-12 — Change 'Filed' messsage (#196)

When Dane submits a task from the Tasks page, the confirmation "Filed SP-n" used to appear as a small line under the toolbar, pushing the Add Task box and the board down a little. Now it pops up as a larger, light-blue pod floating in the middle of the Tasks heading's row, on top of the page, so nothing else moves. It stays for 3 seconds, fades out over 1 second, and disappears.

Warnings are deliberately not in the fading pod: if the project check could not run, or a file was too big to upload, that message still appears as a line under the toolbar and stays until the page is redrawn, so it cannot vanish before he reads it.
