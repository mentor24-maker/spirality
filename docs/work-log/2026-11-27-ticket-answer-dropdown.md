## 2026-11-27 — Answer a card's lettered options from a dropdown on the ticket page (#351)

Some cards ask Dane to choose between lettered options, like "A (the Mini)
or B (a hosted server)". On the phone page he could already pick one from a
dropdown. On the full ticket page he still had to type the letter into the
reply box.

Now the ticket page shows the same dropdown, labelled Your Answer, inside
the blue box on the left, under the red question, with a green Send button.
Picking B and pressing Send posts "B" as his reply, exactly as if he had
typed it. The reply box stays underneath for any answer the options do not
cover. If the post fails, the message bar at the top says so and the
dropdown stays. Once his answer is in, both go away, as the reply box always
has.

Both pages now decide when to offer the dropdown with one shared piece of
code, instead of each having its own copy, so they cannot drift apart.
