## 2026-11-09 — Reformat all tickets (#142)

Ticket titles used to open with a problem, often about Dane being stuck, then
a colon and the fix ("WaveCenter still runs on ClickUp: move it to the
tracker"). Dane asked for titles that just say what the ticket does ("Move
WaveCenter from ClickUp to Spirality tracker"). The rule the loops follow when
they name a ticket now says exactly that: start with a verb, name the thing,
keep it short, put the why in the description. It is written the same way in
the spec, intake and build instructions, in both copies of the loop guide (the
one this project reads and the one new projects are given), and in the example
an outside site sees. Existing tickets keep their names, because an open pull
request's title must match its ticket's name exactly. This is the first ticket
in Dane's series on reformatting tickets.
