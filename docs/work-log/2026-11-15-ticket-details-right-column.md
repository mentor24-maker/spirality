## 2026-11-15 — Show the ticket's details in the right column instead of a Reply box while a loop has it (#251)

When a loop has a ticket (Requested, Queued, Building, In review, Rework or Live), Dane has nothing to answer, but the Task Details page still showed a big "Reply as Dane" box on the right and hid the ticket itself in a closed Details fold underneath.

The Reply box is gone from those six statuses. The right column now shows the ticket itself, already open: its id line, the Change This Ticket panel, the description, attachments and comments. Under the comments is a new Add Comment box ("Add a note for the loop"), so Dane can leave guidance on a Queued ticket and the build loop will read it when it picks the ticket up. Copy to Another Project is still at the foot, with Delete below it when the ticket can still be deleted.

Needs your input and Ready to launch look exactly as before, because those are the pages where Dane does answer. On a phone-width window the two columns stack.

The comment goes through the same route as the reply box, word for word as typed. A failed post says "Not posted" and keeps what he typed.
